Mehran Karimzadeh

I am currently an AI Scientist at Xaira Therapeutics and am based in Seattle, WA. I am interested in leveraging deep learning for personalized medicine applications, particularly in the context of early detection, target discovery, and precision therapy. I have contributed to deep learning models for liquid biopsy and cellular perturbation, including Orion, Exai-1 (cover article, Nature Machine Intelligence 2025), and X-Cell (pre-print 2026).
